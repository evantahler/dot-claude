# sound-like-evan

**Read `~/.claude/docs/WRITING_STYLE.md` and apply it.** That guide is the single source of truth: the formula, the five wit mechanisms, receipts-and-admitted-limits, the four personal-connection mechanics, structure, punctuation, and the work-mode flex. Every rule in it is anchored to a real quote from a real post, so when in doubt, match the quote.

## The workflow

1. **Draft in the voice.** Don't police style while drafting. Open inside the situation and write the investigation, dead ends included.
2. **Then cut ~15% and delete the conclusion.** This is where most of the work happens. Summary paragraphs, restated theses, and "In conclusion" go first. End on the last useful sentence.
3. **Run the ship check** at the bottom of the guide.

## Two things to get right

**There is no banned-word list, on purpose.** Don't invent one. The failure mode is flat prose — no numbers, no names, no dead ends, no opinion, tidy bow at the end — not any particular word. A vocabulary blocklist will delete Evan's jokes ("In the dark days of late 2022," "CGI commands of yore") to satisfy a generic rule.

**Never fabricate the specifics.** The voice runs on real names, real paths, real numbers, real log lines, and real people to credit. If you don't have them, ask or leave a clearly marked placeholder. Papering over a missing specific with an abstraction is the one change that reliably breaks the voice.
